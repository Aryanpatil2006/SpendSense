# SpendWise — Smart Student Expense Tracker

## 1. Problem Statement

Students often have multiple daily expenses such as food, transportation, academic supplies, entertainment, and shopping. These small expenses are difficult to track manually, making it challenging to understand spending patterns and manage a monthly budget.

**SpendWise** provides a simple and visual platform for students to record, categorize, monitor, and analyze their daily expenses.

## 2. Scope of the Project

The project focuses on developing a student-friendly expense tracking application that allows users to:

* Record daily expenses.
* Categorize expenses.
* Edit and delete transactions.
* Track monthly and category-wise budgets.
* View spending patterns through visual analytics.
* Identify unusual spending patterns.
* Import and export expense data using CSV.
* Store expense data locally in the browser.

## 3. Target Users

* College and university students.
* Students managing a monthly allowance.
* Students living away from home.
* Students who want to monitor and understand their daily spending habits.

## 4. High-Level Features

* **Expense Management** — Add, edit, view, and delete expenses.
* **Smart Categorization** — Organize expenses into categories such as Food, Transport, Academic, Entertainment, Shopping, and others.
* **Dashboard** — View total spending, transactions, averages, and category-wise information.
* **Visual Analytics** — Understand spending patterns through charts and visual data.
* **Expense Timeline** — View expenses in chronological order.
* **Spending Heatmap** — Visualize spending activity across different days.
* **Budget Management** — Set and monitor category-wise and monthly budgets.
* **Anomaly Detection** — Identify unusual spending patterns.
* **CSV Import/Export** — Import and export expense records.
* **Local Storage** — Preserve expense data in the browser.
